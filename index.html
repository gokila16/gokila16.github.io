/ABOUT/

Hi! I'm Gokila, a CS master's student at Cal State Long Beach. I like understanding why models behave the way they do, and then making them smaller, faster, and more honest about how well they work. Lately that's meant pruning audio transformers, rethinking GPU memory for LLM inference, and measuring whether LLM-written tests really catch bugs.

I also love shipping things. Before grad school I spent a year and a half at TransUnion building full-stack apps and test automation, and right now I'm building a VS Code extension that teaches you to debug instead of doing it for you.

When I'm away from the keyboard, you'll find me at the beach, wandering around town, people-watching, stargazing, reading novels where fish rain from the sky, or rewatching old cartoons and sitcoms for the hunderedth time. When I'm not away from it, I'm happily staring at my monitor for hours, building something.
